# Third-party notices

The bibliography credits Goresky–Klapper, Lin–Xiao–Chen, Chen–Winterhof,
Boca–Heersink–Spiegelhalter, Heersink, Marklof and the author's earlier
binary paper. Corollary 7.2 credits Kanold's classical Jacobsthal bound
as reported by Costello–Watts, arXiv:1208.5342v2, Introduction pp. 1–2.
Their publications are cited and linked, not redistributed.

and survival-integral identities are classical tools. The specific mean
inequalities and computational finite premises are explained in the paper.

were developed for the present research; the prime reference panel is an
author-generated finite table, not a copied third-party dataset.
No third-party software implementation or full-text paper is bundled.

LaTeX packages come from the reader's existing TeX distribution.
