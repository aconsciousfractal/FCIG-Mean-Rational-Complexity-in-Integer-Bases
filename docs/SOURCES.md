# Sources and comparison

This is a source/version guide, not exhaustive novelty certification.
The paper bibliography supplies the references used in its proofs and context.
Classical methods, imported theorems and the specific new mean inequalities
are distinguished.

| Source | Exact accessible version and use |
|---|---|
| Costello–Watts, A computational upper bound on Jacobsthal's function | [arXiv1208.5342v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.5342v2), Introduction pp.1–2. Accessible source for Kanold's bound J(q)<=2^omega(q) for every integer with omega(q) distinct prime factors. Its consecutive-interval function equals our cyclic unit gap. Original Kanold1967 proof was not inspected; no primorial domination is assumed. Supplies the imported gap bound in Corollary7.2, not an antecedent claim for the rational-height mean theorem. |
| Goresky–Klapper, Algebraic Shift Register Sequences | Cambridge2012, [DOI10.1017/CBO9781139057448](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139057448); [author draft2009](https://www.cs.uky.edu/~klapper/pdf/algebraic.pdf). Section locators19.3/21.3/21.5 refer to the draft read;19.1 has a different connection convention. General leading lower coefficient already has antecedents. |
| Lin–Xiao–Chen | AIMS Mathematics11(7)(2026),20267–20290, [DOI10.3934/math.2026823](https://doi.org/10.3934/math.2026823). Question3.2 asks for a general upper; compare explicitly with the full-unit rational-height definition. |
| Earlier binary paper | Babanskyy, [version0.2.0 repository](https://github.com/aconsciousfractal/FCIG-Exact-Mean-Bound-for-the-Rational-Complexity-of-Binary-Strings). Exact binary mean theorem imported; binary distribution/variance are not new claims of this article. |
| Chen–Winterhof | [arXiv2501.16785v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16785v1) is the accessible preprint comparison. Final DCC93(2025),2191–2203, [DOI10.1007/s10623-025-01592-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-025-01592-1): abstract/metadata accessible, final body not read. No lemma-by-lemma version equivalence asserted. |
| Boca–Heersink–Spiegelhalter | [arXiv1301.0277v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.0277v2), Integers13(2013),A44. Coprime-denominator Farey-gap antecedent; its gap observable is not automatically this modular max-height minimum. |
| Heersink | [arXiv1712.03258v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03258v2), ETDS41(2021),471–493. Congruence-cover methods are relevant context; no unverified effective rate imported. |
| Marklof, Smallest denominators | [arXiv2310.11251v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11251v2), BLMS56(2024),1920–1938, [DOI10.1112/blms.13034](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.13034). Unmarked small-denominator/discrete-sampling antecedent, with its own observable. |
| Marklof, The log moments of smallest denominators | [arXiv2312.15303v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15303v2), Integers24(2024),A55, [DOI10.5281/zenodo.12167470](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12167470). General moment method and explicit unmarked constants; no copied mixed-height constant. |

Additional comparison: [Han–Lee2507.05905v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05905v2)
studies congruence-restricted primitive lattice-count moments. Count moments
do not alone supply this full-unit square-window void law, finite modular
sample or exact mean inequality. This is a model comparison, not proof that
no overlap exists.

No priority claim depends on a journal abstract. No full third-party text,
source PDF or figure is bundled. [AI-use disclosure](../AI_USE.md) and
[limits](KNOWN_LIMITS.md) describe review/source boundaries.
